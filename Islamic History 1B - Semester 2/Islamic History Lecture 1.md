### The Crusades

- c.1096-c.1229 CE
- A series of holy wars fought by European Christians to 'free' the Holy Land from Muslim rule; triggered by the Seljuk conquest of Christian Byzantium
- Anachronistic interpretations often portray it as 'a clash of civilisations' (the phrase having been made popular by Samuel P. Huntington's book of the same name)
- In a contemporary setting, the crusades are often used by radical and extremist groups, both Muslim-affiliated and otherwise

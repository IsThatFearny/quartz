### Settled Societies
</br>

- Settled societies were largely agrarian, and the state's primary source of wealth was agriculture
- All sources from these societies were written by the elites (top 10% of the population) and by men
- Travel over land consisted of caravans, and by sea the primary medium was coastal navigation

</br>

### Nomadic Societies

- Nomadic societies were largely tribal, organised around a common ancestor (whether real or apocryphal)
- They generally have no written history (aside from the occasional mention in Greek, Persian, or Chinese sources)
- Thus, these societies are mostly known from the archaeological record
- Nomadic and semi-nomadic civilisation was very important for trade with the settled world:
	- The Turks traded cattle, slaves, and furs
	- The Berbers and Arabs usually traded spices
- However, they were also known as raiders, attacking the settled world

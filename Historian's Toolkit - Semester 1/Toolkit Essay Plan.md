### Do we distort LGBTQ+ history by treating the Stonewall Riots as a moment of radical change?

#### Yes:

- Ignores prior homophile movements and banalises their role in promoting queer civil rights
- Must question whether the movement would have continued as such without stonewall

</br>

#### No:

- Stonewall *was* a moment of radical change, made possible by previous activism

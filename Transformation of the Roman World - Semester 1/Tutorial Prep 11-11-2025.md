- Who was Maximus the confessor and why was he arrested
	- Educated in Constantinople
	- Left public life and became a monk in Chrysopolis
	- Became abbot of the monastery
	- Fled to a monastery near Carthage after Persian conquest
	- Arrested for his opposition to the Monothelite doctrine by Constans II (monothelitism being the belief that there are two natures of Christ, divine and human, united within him;Christ however has no divine will, only human will)

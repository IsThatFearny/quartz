### *Agamemnon Chronology*

- The watchman sees the beacon lit and proclaims victory at Troy (Ilium)- Clytaemestra does likewise (letting out a 'cry of victory')
- A herald arrives, proclaiming the greatness of Agamemnon and Menelaus
- Clytaemestra reveals that Orestes is being raised by another for fear of plots against him in his father's absence
- Agamemnon returns, and briefly quarrels with Clytaemestra about how she should praise him; he emphasises that he remains mortal and thus should not be lauded as she does, but she convinces him that if Priam (king of Troy) had won such a victory, he would be treated the same - Agamemnon should accept honour
- Agamemnon introduces Cassandra, his Trojan concubine gifted to him by the army
- Cassandra refuses to exit the carriage despite encouragement from Clytaemestra - when the chorus convince her to, she steps out and, amid cries of appeal and questioning to Apollo, prophesises the death of Agamemnon by his wife's hand

### Social History


> [!quote] W. Conze, 'Social History' (1967)
> "Social history means the history of society or, more precisely, of social structures, processes, and trends. For this reason, it is involved not only with the field of history, but also with that of sociology"

</br>

### Contemporary Concerns

- 'Every history is contemporary history' - every time we write history, we are inevitably influenced by the historical period that we are living in and by the culture that surrounds us. It is impossible to write a history that is devoid of contemporary concerns
- There are certain periods in which the academic culture is more interested in race, gender, etc. etc.
- Superimposing one's own convictions onto the response that one gives to a historical question is not a legitimate form of writing

### Becoming Scientific


> [!quote] S. Hin (2013)
> “… many of us see the future of ancient history as one in which interdisciplinary explorations take in a prominent place, and one in which ‘interdisciplinary’ is defined rather more broadly than it used to be: as encompassing findings and methods from disciplines as ‘exotic’ as climate research, genetics, medicine, economics, anthropology and biology”.

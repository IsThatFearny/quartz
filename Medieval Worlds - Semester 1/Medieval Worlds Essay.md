### Was long-distance travel basically a matter of setting out into the unknown during the Middle Ages?

There are various accounts of long-distance travel throughout the Middle Ages, ranging from pilgrimages, to missionary work, to diplomacy. Long-distance travel was certainly an arduous task, with many challenges and climes to combat; but whether it was a matter of 'setting out into the unknown' is greatly debated. The traditional conception of the people of the Middle Ages as "confined within limited geographical horizons", whilst mostly considered to be inaccurate, still proliferates, perhaps even subconsciously, amongst scholarship.[^1]

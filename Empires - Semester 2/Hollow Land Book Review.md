*Hollow Land* is Eyal Weizman's investigation into the Israeli regime of occupation, apartheid, and imperial expansion, with a specific focus on Weizman's speciality of architecture and urban planning. The book details how the Israeli regime has utilised architecture to maintain and expand their occupation of Palestine (henceforth referred to as the Occupied Territories), as well as their domination of the Palestinian people. Wiezman elaborates on the five architectural components which are the foundational tenets of the occupation: the contested capital of Jerusalem, Israeli settlements, 'border' fortifications, checkpoints between Israel and the Gaza strip/West Bank, the Wall, the evacuation of Gaza, and finally targeted assassinations.

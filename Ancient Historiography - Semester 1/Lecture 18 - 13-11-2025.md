## Cassius Dio

### Biography

- c. 165-235 CE
- from Nicaea in Bithynia (modern turkey)
- born into wealth (senatorial family)
- in rome by 180; followed the usual *cursus honorum* (natural way to rise through public office)
- consul twice, second time with emperor Alexander Severus (229)
- governs provinces between 218-230
- returns to Bithynia late in Alexander Severus' reign
- member of the emperor's *consilium* in an advisory role

### Dio's Lifetime

- 161-180: reign of Marcus Aurelius  
- 180-192: reign of Commodus, murdered 31 Dec 192  
- 193: reigns of Pertinax (1 Jan–28 Mar); Didius Julianus (29 Mar–1 Jun)  
- 197-211: reign of Septimius Severus  
- 193-197: civil war  
- 198: Sept. Severus’ son Caracalla (10) becomes co-emperor  
- 209: younger son Geta becomes co-emperor w/Severus & Caracalla  
- 211: death of Severus (4 Feb); murder of Geta (26 Dec)  
- 211-217: sole reign of Caracalla, murdered 8 Apr 217  
- 212: Constitutio Antoniniana (extension of Roman citizenship)  
- 217-218: reign of Macrinus (not a Severan), executed  
- 218-222: reign of Elagabalus, murdered 13 Mar 222  
- 222-235: reign of Alexander Severus, murdered Mar 235

</br>

### Dio's Works

- *Roman History*
	- 80 books, from the foundation of Rome to 229 CE
	- annalistic
	- written in Greek
	- 11 manuscripts, but text is piecemeal
	- surviving books:
		- 36-54: 68-10 BCE
		- some of 55-60: 9 BCE - 46 CE
		- some of 79-80: 217-220 CE
	- excerpts made in the 10<sup>th</sup> century directly cite dio
	- the rest are in epitomes by Xiphilinus (1070s) and Zonaras (1118)

</br>

### Dio's process

- ten years of researching, twelve years of writing
- selective when writing
- current events also recorded
- if an eyewitness, details were usually narrated fully despite sometimes being trivial

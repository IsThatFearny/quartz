## Thucydides

- Records events of a war that he himself participated in  
- Reports on what he considers most worthy of mention  
- Intends for his account to be of value for the future  
- Intends to report „the exact truth“ of what happened and takes pains to establish what that was  
- Distinguishes between speeches = logoi (writes what he or other eye witnesses remember and adds what he believes would have been said at the occasion) and deeds/events = erga (tries to establish the exact truth of what happened)  
- Reports on events in a chronological order and uses different dating systems to offer a synchronised narration accessible to a pan-Hellenic audience  
- Narrates events from a commander’s perspective  
- Offers not only a report on events but also an analysis

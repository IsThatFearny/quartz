### The Ghaznavids (977-1186)

- The first Muslim Turkish dynasty
- Founded by a Turkish general of slave origin
- Took its name from its capital, Ghazna (located in modern Afghanistan)
- Had a Persian administration and court culture
- Ghaznavid descendants retreated to Lahore after the Seljuqs arrived in the region
- The Ghaznavids were well placed to lead various incursions into India, being attracted by its wealth
- These incursions were presented as *jihad*
- Elephants were used by the Ghaznavids for military purposes, such as in their battles against the Seljuqs
- There was an Indian quarter in the capital, Ghazna, in which the Indians were in charge of keeping the elephants
- Some campaigns into India were made solely for the purpose of capturing elephants for use in transportation, siege, and on the battlefield

### Islam in India

- The indigenous religions/philosophies in India included:
	- Hinduism
	- Vedas
	- Yoga
	- Buddhism
	- Jainism
- India practised the caste class system, by which one's caste was immutable and one could not change social class via any means
- Some aspects of Hinduism were similar to Sufism, which spread in South Asia from the 12th century onwards
- Muslim settlers, originally settling for the purpose of trade, built mosques and attracted Islamic scholars (*ulama*). These scholars taught their religion to locals
- People tended to convert to Sunni Islam and Shi'i Islam (both Twelver and Isma'ili form) 

</br>

### The Ghurids (c. 1150-1206)

- Named after the region of 'Ghur'
- Originating from Iran
- Overthrew Ghaznavid overlords in 1187, taking Lahore in 1186 and Delhi in 1193

</br>

### The Aybeg Delhi Sultanate (1206-1290)

**The First Delhi Sultanate**

A real sultanate is established under the first major Aybeg ruler, Iltutmish (r. 1211-1236). Iltutmish, a turkic name meaning 'holder to power', repelled the Mongol invasion, and attracted Turko-Mongol tribal migrations in the region.

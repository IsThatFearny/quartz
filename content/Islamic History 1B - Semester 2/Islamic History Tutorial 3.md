## Prep

  
1. According to El-Azhari, were Dayfa of Aleppo and Shajar al-Durr of Egypt queens in their own right? 

Both Dayfa of Aleppo and Shajar al-Durr were indeed recognised as Queens of their respective kingdoms, but their power was diluted somewhat as a result of the strength of the Atabegs who surrounded them. The atabegs in question possessed control of military forces and also exercised significant political influence over those who controlled various aspects of the kingdoms.

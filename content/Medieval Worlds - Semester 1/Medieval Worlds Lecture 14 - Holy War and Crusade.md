# Medieval Worlds Lecture 14 - Holy War and Crusade

- Crusades were set apart from other campaigns with the promise of spiritual awards granted by Popes (e.g. promises of heaven, forgiveness of sins, etc.)
- In late 1000s, the Byzantine empire was losing significant amounts of land to the Seljuks - the First Crusade (1099) is partly a response to this
- Before the First Crusade, there were various, smaller 'proto-crusades', in which the Pope would grant indulgences to small groups (i.e. the Normans, Italians, etc.) to fight in smaller areas and recapture land that the Caliphate possessed in the Mediterranean

</br>

### What caused the First Crusade?

- At the Battle of Manzikert in 1071, the Seljuk Turks moved into Asia Minor
- There was some discussion of a campaign at the Council of Piacenza in March 1095
- The pope then preached a campaign at the Council of Clermont in November 1095
- Urban II launched the First Crusade at Clermont for various reasons

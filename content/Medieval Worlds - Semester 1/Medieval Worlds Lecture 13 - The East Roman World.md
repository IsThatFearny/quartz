# Medieval Worlds Lecture 13 - The East Roman World


- Only the 'western' part of the Roman Empire fell in the late 5th-century - the 'east' (i.e. modern day Turkey, Georgia, Egypt, etc.) continues
- Unbroken institutional continuity from the fall of Rome to the Crusades
- Constantinople (i.e. Istanbul) became the 'new Rome'
- East Roman empire is able to reconquer areas of the western Roman Empire (such as Italy, south Spain, etc.) by 555 AD
- In the 7th-century, 2/3 of the East Roman Empire is conquered by the Islamic Caliphates

# The Julio-Claudians

</br>

- Belonged to the Julian or Claudian family (or both)
</br>

- Augustus: r. 27 BCE - 14 CE
	- Great-Nephew and adopted son of J. Caesar
-  Tiberius: r. 14 - 37 CE
	- Stepson and adopted son of Augustus
- Gaius (Caligula): r. 37 - 41 CE
	- Great-nephew and adopted son of Tiberius; great-grandson of Augustus
- Claudius: r. 41 - 54 CE
	- Uncle of Caligula; nephew of Tiberius
- Nero: r. 54 - 68 CE
	- Great-nephew, stepson, and adopted son of Claudius; great-great-grandson of Augustus

</br>

### Main Historical Sources

- Augustus: *Res Gestae* ('Things done')
- Philo of Alexandria, c. 20 BCE - c. 50 CE: *Embassy to Gaius*
- Tacitus, c. 56 - c. 120 CE: *Annals* and *Histories*
- Suetonius, c.69 - after 122 CE: *Lives of the Caesars*
- Dio Cassius c. 165 - c. 235 CE: *Roman History*

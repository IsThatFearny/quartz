**What was Gregory's attitude towards the Franks and their kings?**

Gregory saw the franks as idolatrous and blasphemous. Gregory criticises Childeric, their king, as being "hopelessly undone by wantonness" and states that he "began to violate [the Franks'] daughters". There is a great emphasis on their 'errors of Paganism' when discussing the kings of the Franks - this makes sense, as Gregory was a devout Christian. Gregory's account suggests that political life was rather volatile during the Merovingian era, as well as being steeped in religious practices such as pagan rituals and conversion to Christianity.

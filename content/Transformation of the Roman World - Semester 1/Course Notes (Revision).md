## Political Structures and Geography in the New Roman Empire

</br>

### The third-century crisis

- AD 235-284
- Crisis on both eastern and western frontiers
- Civil War/Plague/Religious tensions
- Currency devaluation + economic crisis - denarii and antoniniani lost physical value in reduced silver content; aurei lost gold content
- Geopolitical shifts
- A **ton** of emperors and a **ton** of usurpers
- In the east: Sasanian Persian invasions; In the west: Germanic and gothic invasions
- 'Soldier emperors' AD 268-285: Claudius II Gothicus, Aurelian, Tacitus, Florianus, Probus, Carus, Carinus, and Numerian
#### **Diocletian**

- Born in Dalmatia as Gaius Valerius Diocles
- Middle-class family
- Brilliant military career, reaching the post of commander of the imperial bodyguard
- Assumes power and changes name after the death of Numerian
- Defeats Carinus (Numerian's brother) and becomes sole ruler

**Reforms**

- Governance and Succession
	- July 285 - Diocletian appoints Maximian, his long-term friend as Caesar, who would remain loyal to Diocletian throughout his life
	- Diocletian keeps the Eastern half of the empire - Maximian rules the west
	- Maximian promoted to Augustus in April 286 but remains subordinate to Diocletian
	- Constantius and Galerius appointed as Caesars in March 293; Constantius to work with Maximian, Galerius with Diocletian - each is married to the daughter/step-daughter of their Augustus
- Provincial administration
	- Existing provinces divided into much smaller units
	- Italy loses fiscal exemption and divided up
	- Provinces grouped into 12-15 dioceses by early fourth century
	- Later in the fourth century, empire is divided into three/four prefectures under praetorian prefects
	- Provincial governors have only civil responsibilities: administration of justice and collection of taxes
	- Military is separated from civil administration
	- Late Roman pyramid of power: Imperial college > 3/4 praetorian prefects > 12/15 diocesan vicars > 120 provincial governors
	- Emperors are extremely mobile, residing in a residence city near to the frontiers
	- Central administration of the government is secured by the *comitatus*, a group of officials who constantly accompany emperors
- Economic policy
	- Inflation a major problem - scarcity of precious metal

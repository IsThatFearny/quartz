### Is 'history from below' a reflection of the theories of Marx and Engels?

</br>

Whilst 'history from below' has its roots in pre-Marxist scholarship, its actual genesis was heavily influenced by the theories of Marx and Engels.[^1] Scholars such as E.P. Thompson, Eric Hobsbawm, and Raphael Samuel all had a Marxist or Communist background, and practised history using Marxist methodology. However, modern 'history from below' or 'people's history' has developed significantly since the scholarship of the mid-20<sup>th</sup> century and has diverged significantly in both methodology and ethos. One cannot therefore argue that 'history from below' remains true to its Marxist roots as its modern form has evolved too far to remain identifiable with Marxism, and this essay contends this position.

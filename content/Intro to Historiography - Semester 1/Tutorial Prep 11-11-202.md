Bring to the class one item (it could be a photograph, but could equally well be a text, a painting, an object, etc.) from any period in history that captures something of the idea of ‘Orientalism’ as articulated by Said. Come prepared to present it (briefly) to the group. When and where was it produced, by whom, and for what purpose? In what sense can it be described as ‘Orientalist’?

'The Snake Charmer' - used on the original cover of *Orientalism*. Painted by French artist Jean-Leon Gerome in 1879. Conflates Ottoman Turkey and Egypt. Uses Islamic stereotypes to bring in various aspects of 'orientalism' even though they may not actually have co-existed.

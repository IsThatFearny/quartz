## Empires of Space and Imagination

- "Colonialism" = the act of setting up colonies, territories, protectorates, etc. and asserting command over subjugated populations
- "Imperialism" = the ideological force behind the actions of colonialism
- Why do we study this? Colonialism extremely important in Anthro as the two are inherently interlinked; anthropologists initially engaged in colonialism in order to reinforce imperial control over an indigenous people. Anthro study includes:
	- Cultural rep. of non-European 'others'
	- Exploitation of resources for colonial projects
	- Relationship between coloniser and colonised
	- Reactions to colonialism
- 'Race' is a colonially-produced social reality that was originally concerned not with inclusion, but with exclusion of racial 'others'
	- Science has always been used as a way to expand colonial outreach - race was used as a scientific method of subjugation

### Ideology of Empire and Technology through the Space Race

- Russian empire not particularly interested in resource extraction, more interested in land expansion. Not necessarily 'nation-state' based originally, more soviet-based
- Space race was the physical manifestation of cold war tensions - space was a way of thinking about the possibilities of how far power could spread (e.g. weapons, satellites, etc.) 
- Operation Paperclip: highly classified introduction of former Nazi scientists into the US space programme, with NASA complicit in hiding the pasts of these individuals
- Sputnik: launched October 4 1957, the first artificial satellite to orbit the earth
- Space considered the 'real' *terra nullius* (colonial term applied to indigenous peoples in order to dehumanise them and 'justify' subjugation of these peoples)
- Colonial perspective on space exploration mirrored what was occurring on earth

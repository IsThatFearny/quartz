### List of differences

- Gallis est omnis divisa in partes tres
	- "*All of gaul* is divided into three parts"
	- ''Gaul is *a whole* divided into three parts"
	- "Gaul *comprises three areas*"



- quarum unam incolunt Belgae
	- "one of which the Belgae inhabit"
	- "one of which is *inhabited by* the Belgae"
	- "inhabited *respectively* by the Belgae"



- aliam Aquitani
	- "another the Aquitani"
	- "another *by the* Aquitani"
	- "the Aquitani"



- tertiam qui ipsorum lingua Celtae
	- "and the third those who *in their own language* are called Celts"
	- "and a third by a people *called in their own tongue* Celtae"
	- "and a people who *call themselves* Celts"



- nostra Galli appellantur
	- "in our language Gauls"
	- "in the Latin Galli"
	- "though we call them Gauls"



- Hi omnes lingua, institutis, legibus inter se differunt
	- "All these differ from each other in language, customs, and laws"
	- "All these are different one from another in language, institutions, and laws"
	- "All of these have different languages, customs, and laws"



- Gallos ab Aquitanis Garumna flumen
	- "The Garonne river separates the Gauls from the Aquitani"
	- "The Galli (Gauls) are separated from the Aquitani by the river Garonne"
	- "The Celts are separated from the Aquitani by the river Garonne"



- a Belgis Matrona et Sequana dividit
	- "the Marne and the Seine separate them from the Belgae"
	- "from the Belgae by the Marne and the Seine"
	- "from the Belgae by the Marne and Seine"



- Horum omnium fortissimi sunt Belgae
	- "Of all these, the Belgae are the strongest"
	- "Of all these peoples the Belgae are the most courageous"
	- "The Belgae are the bravest of the three peoples"



- propterea quod a cultu atque humanitate provinciae longissime absunt
	- "because they are the farthest removed from the refined and civilised province"
	- "because they are the farthest removed from the culture and the civilisation of the province"
	- "being farthest removed from the highly developed civilisation of the Roman Province"



- minimeque ad eos mercartores saepe commeant atque ea quae ad effeminandos animos pertinent important
	- "and merchants least frequently resort to them, bringing those things which tend to soften their warlike spirit"
	- "and least often visited by merchants introducing the commodities that make for effeminacy"
	- "least often visited by merchants with enervating luxuries for sale"



- proximique sunt Germanis, qui trans Rhenum incolunt, quibuscum continenter bellum gerunt
	- "and they are nearest to the Germans, who live across the Rhine, with whom they continually wage war"
	- "and also because they are nearest to the Germans dwelling beyond the Rhine, with whom they are continually at war"
	- "and nearest to the Germans across the Rhine, with whom they are continually at war"



- Quade de causa Helvetii quoque reliquos Gallas virtute praecedunt
	- "For this reason, the Helvetii also surpass the other Gauls in valour"
	- "For this cause the Helvetii also excel the rest of the Gauls in valour"
	- "For the same reason the Helvetii are braver than the rest of the Celts"



- quod fere cotidianis proeliis cum Germanis contendunt
	- "as they almost daily contend with the Germans"
	- "because they are struggling in almost daily fights with the Germans"
	- "they are in almost daily conflict with the Germans"



- cum aut suis finibus eos prohibent aut ipsi in eorum finibus bellum gerunt

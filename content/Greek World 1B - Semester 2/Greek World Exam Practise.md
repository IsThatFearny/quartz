**What does the Periclean building programme tell us about the socio-economic  
and political situation of Athens? Discuss the art and architecture as part of your  
evidence.**

### Intro

- Large scale building program during the Peloponnesian war
- Primarily focused on the Acropolis (Temple of Athena Nike, Parthenon, etc.)
- Had a high cost (a great deal of gold and marble used)
- Such high cost might imply that Athens was in a strong financial position, although this may not have been the case

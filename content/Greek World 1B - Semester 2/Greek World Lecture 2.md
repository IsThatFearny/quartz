### When did the Delian League turn into an empire?

Harold Mattingly makes the argument that the league became an empire between Pericles and Cleon (basing his argument on the writings of Thucydides). Pericles was a more moderate leader, whereas Cleon was an imperialist demagogue

#### Evolution of Language

In various decrees throughout this period, there was a clear change in the language used to describe the league/developing empire: from 'the alliance' to 'Athens and her allies', and then to 'the cities that the Athenians rule'

#### The Three Bar Sigma Controversy

The Three Bar Sigma was originally thought to date a source as pre-445 BC (as from 445 BC onwards a four bar sigma was used). However, this theory was proven false in 1990, showing that:

- Epigraphic features cannot be treated as decisive in establishing a chronology
- All decrees featuring 'harsh' imperialistic language may need to be downdated to the era of Cleon
- Imperial activities were a reality during the age of Pericles, and even earlier, e.g. oaths of loyalty, Athenian intervention in external politics, suppression of dissent, etc.

</br>

The rule of Pericles was generally harsh, and there was a turn amongst the Greeks towards Sparta, who had promised liberation. It became impossible for Athens to dissolve the empire without incurring the wrath of those who felt that they had been wronged or suppressed by the Athenians.

### Athenian Democracy

#### Main Sources

- *The Athenaion Politeia* of the School of Aristotle
- *The Athenian Constitution* by the 'Old Oligarch'
- Attic orators, mainly 4th century
- Attic inscriptions

</br>

#### Development of Democracy

- Solon: archonship 594 BC
- The reforms of Cleisthenes: 510-508 BC
- The introduction of ostracism: 488 BC
- The reforms of Ephialtes: 462 BC
- Reconciliation and revision of the law code: 403 BC

</br>

Athens changed their constitution **eleven** times before 'democracy' came into being

##### The Reforms of Cleisthenes

Cleisthenes introduced a legislative body, the **Council of 500**. It included 50 members per tribe. A form of devolution was also introduced: 10 tribes, each with 3 trittyes (larger regions; one city, one coastal, and one inland); and 139 demes (village/local community equivalents), distributed across the trittyes.

# Greek World Lecture 5

- Most other Greek societies aside from Athens were oligarchies (e.g. Sparta, where power was held by the military elite) or autocracies/monarchies
- 'Demos' (as in 'mob') was originally demeaning
- The Athenian form of democracy was more direct than the modern form - it was not representative
- Attitudes towards democracy in its infancy tended to be hostile - Athenian democracy was unique and exceptional in the ancient world

> [!quote] Theseus (in Euripides' *Supplices*)

No, it is free, for here the people rule...
>

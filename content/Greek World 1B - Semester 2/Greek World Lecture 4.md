### The Pediments

- The pediment of the Parthenon (the end face of the roof structure) depicts a contest between Athena and Poseidon to decide who will be the patron deity of the city. Each presents a gift to the city in order to win the favour of the people of Athens. Poseidon throws his spear onto the acropolis, and creates a salt spring; Athena manifests an olive tree on the Acropolis. Athena is voted to be the winner. Surrounding this scene are legends of Athens' past
- Artemis is depicted delivering the news of the result of the contest, and is one of the best surviving depictions
- Artemis' clothing styling (wet drapery style, adhering against her body) is characteristic of the 430s
- Also depicted (on the opposite pediment) is the birth of Athena, who is born from the split in Zeus' skull caused by Hephaestus. This pediment was badly damaged in 1687 when a shell hit the Parthenon; and was also damaged when the building was turned into a church, requiring cutting into the side of the building, badly damaging the pediment

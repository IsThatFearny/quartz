The first 'actor', Thespis, moved away from monologue performances in 530BC, including dialogue and rhetoric in the shows that were staged.

A shrine/temple was built to house the cult of *Dionysos Eluthereos* (Dionysos the Liberator) - the cult statue was brought to the Acropolis in a small temple on the south slope (parts of this temple remain to this day).

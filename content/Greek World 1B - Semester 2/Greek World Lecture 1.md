### Origins of the Athenian Empire

- Athenians rebuilt the city and its walls (in order to keep the 'barbarians' which had plagued them so recently out)
- Themistocles encouraged the building of the Peiraeus, in order to develop Athens into a naval power (this would be key in future conquests against the Persians and otherwise)
- Spartan withdrawal of leadership of the Greek powers against the Persians allowed Athens to take over the hegemony, leading the alliance; Athens had control over which cities should provide money, which should provide ships, etc.
- The Athenians instituted the office of 'Treasures to the Greeks' (*Hellenotamiai*), which was responsible for receiving the tribute (*phoros*). The treasury was on the island of Delos, and this was where the allied powers met
- Aristides, son of Lysimachus, prompted the Ionian's defection from the Spartan alliance
- In the intervening period between the Persian War and the Peloponnesian War, Athens went from leading autonomous allies with common policy, to having significant control over politics, management of war, and finances
- Delian league established in 478 BC, with (allegedly) anti-Persian purposes

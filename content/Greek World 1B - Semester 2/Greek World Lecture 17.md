### Alexander the Great's speech at Opis

In this speech to his men, Alexander praises his father - he lauds his generosity and leadership, his military prowess and ability to turn an uncivilised society into a more civilised one. Philip made Macedonia a state of significance which could match the power of the barbarians that had previously terrorised her.

### Philipian narratives

- Diodorus, Book 16
- Justin, Epitome of Pompeius Trogus

Both of these were written later than Philip's reign, and were not specific to Philip himself. In contrast to this, Demosthenes and Aeschines (Athenian orators) were contemporary writers

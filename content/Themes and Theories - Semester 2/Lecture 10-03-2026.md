## Mobility

</br>

### Walking

- Limits settlement size and territory - 80% of Greek poleis estimated to have territory of < 200km<sup>2</sup> (circle with radius of 8km). Agricultural workers could live in the centre and walk out
- Travellers between towns also walked (e.g. Pausanius did much of his travelling on foot). Approx. speed of 3mph
- Alexander's soldiers marched to India - 17k miles
- Reasonable estimate for the Roman army - 20 miles per day

</br>

### Horses and Riding

- Sign of status and wealth
- Signifying social class (e.g. hippeis in Athens, equestrian class in Rome)
- No stirrups used, until 1<sup>st</sup> century BC no saddles
- Donkeys and mules far cheaper to use for basic jobs

</br>

### By Water

- Significantly faster to travel by water than by land
- Bulk transport possible on boats, of commodities or people
- Initial outlay costs
- Seasonal - April-Sept
- Limited to water - landlocked areas not possible

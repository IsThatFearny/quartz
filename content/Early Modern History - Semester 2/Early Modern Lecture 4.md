### Demography, Urbanisation, and Trade

- Urbanisation between 1400-1800 was a slow process when compared to urbanisation post-1800 - less than 2% of the 900 million strong world population in 1800 lived in cities of 100,000 or more.
- A few cities did reach 'modern' sizes, such as London, Beijing, Tokyo, etc.
- Rises in population densities tended to be in areas where trade was concentrated, e.g. Cairo, Amsterdam, etc.
- Social diversity in terms of university acceptance was wide-ranging, and students came from a wide range of social backgrounds
- Significant shifts in technology occurred during the period
- Shipping largely followed coastlines (hence the detail on coastlines on maps) - Galleys (ships with oars) were still used to transport goods, which gave rise to slave trading patterns

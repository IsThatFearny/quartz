- One story of the development of population is a story of sustained growth - as economic conditions improved (improved harvest techniques, better living conditions, etc.), population improved
- There is an alternative story of development, which is that the 'little ice age' caused a decline in world population, resulting from poor harvests and the resulting food shortages

</br>

Both Europeans and the Ottomans had a major stake in the south-east Asian spice trade. Ottoman imperial expansion can therefore be seen as a way of protecting the spice routes which were so valuable to Ottoman trade.

Having been forced out of the spice routes, Europeans began to improve their trading and shipping technology, as well as developing diplomatic bureaucracy such as giving royal licenses to various trading companies (e.g. the East India Company). As a result, major trading hubs such as ports were given improved infrastructure and made defensible, as were ships, making trade significantly more efficient and safer for all parties.

</br>

**Indian Trading**

Indian traders began to learn to use European influence and protection in order to serve their own interests, improving their ability to trade around the world and with greater efficiency.

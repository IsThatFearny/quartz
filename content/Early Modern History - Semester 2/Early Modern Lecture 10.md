# Early Modern Lecture 10

- Although there is a common misconception that the Mughal Empire introduced Islam to the 'western' world, Islam was actually around for many centuries before Mughal imperial control. They were, however, partially responsible for spreading the religion across the world
- Although most rulers were Muslim, this did not mean that their subjects were Muslim - in the case of the Mughals, all rulers were Muslim but the majority of the population were Hindu
- Often the Islamic empires are credited with bringing all faiths and cultures together under the banner of Islam, generally avoiding forcible conversion and accommodating various different religions
- Three primary Islamic empires:
	- Ottoman Empire c. 1300-1922 (The Fall of Constantinople c. 1453)
	- Safavid Empire c. 1501-1722
	- Mughal Empire c. 1526-1857
- These empires had significant crossover (as can be seen by the dates of their existence). From 1526-1722, all three existed at the same time

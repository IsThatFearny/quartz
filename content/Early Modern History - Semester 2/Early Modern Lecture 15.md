- Goods were not just flowing from Asia to Europe - commodities moved throughout the globe
- Historically, Europe was not always the centre of trade. Asia, the Middle East, and South America were also important in commerce
- Heightened across the Pacific and Atlantic in the early modern era was primarily as a result of the Spanish empire. The spaniards would travel from Seville to Mexico city or Lima, and would then travel to Manila in the Philippines (all were Spanish controlled territories, although Manila was already a trade hub with many other south east Asian countries)

### Timeline 

- 1492: Columbus’ arrival in the Caribbean 
- 1494: Treaty of Tordesillas 
- 1498: Vasco da Gama’s arrival in India  
- 1519 - 1521: ‘Conquest’ of Mexico
- 1532: ‘Conquest’ of Peru
- 1519 - 1522: Magellan Circumnavigation  
- 1565: Spanish arrival in the Philippines  
- 1571: Establishment of colony in Manila

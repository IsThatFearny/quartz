### Captain Cook

- Made three separate voyages to the Pacific: 1768-71, 1772-75, 1776-79
- Discovered the east coast of Australia and Hawaii
- First voyage was to Tahiti to chart the transit of Venus
- HIs journals were best sellers, contributing to the popular European imagination of the pacific
- Cook was killed on a Hawaiian beach, murdered by the native population

#### Cook's death

- Humanitarian?
	- Killed trying to stop his marines firing
	- Victim of violent 'savages'
- Cook as a Hawaiian deity
	- His visits coincided with the makahiki festival
	- Appeared at the beginning and returned at the end - he had to be sacrificed
- Cook as a belligerent coloniser
	- His murder was a just retribution of Hawaiians
	- Anti-colonial response to violence

### Second Temple Jewish History

#### Time Periods

- First Temple Period: c. 960 BCE-586 BCE - Temple destroyed by the Babylonians
- Babylonian Exile: period of exile from the kingdom of Judah
- Second Temple Period: c. 515 BCE- 70 CE - destroyed by the romans
- Post-Second Temple Period: after Second Temple, first rabbinic corpus c. 200 CE

#### The Temple

- Central institution in Jerusalem - existed before the Roman Empire
- Sacrifices only made in the temple
- The 'real' dwelling place of the Jewish god
- Payment of the Temple Tax by Jews throughout the Roman Empire

#### Key features (as noted by outsiders)

- Monotheistic, worshipped in the Temple at Jerusalem
- Aniconic - ban on imagery
- Adherence to 'ancestral law' (Torah) - Sabbath observance and dietary laws

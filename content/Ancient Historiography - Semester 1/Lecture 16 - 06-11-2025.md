### The *Histories*: An Overview

- Originally 18 books; 4 (and a bit) survive
- Jan 69 CE - 96 CE
	- Book 1: Assassination of Galba; Otho's manoeuvrings
	- Book 2: Otho vs Vitellius; Battle of Bedriacum; Otho's suicide
	- Book 3: Vespasian vs Vitellius; second Battle of Bedriacum; chaos in Rome; Vitellius' downfall
	- Book 4: Senate and Roman society; Civilis' revolt
	- Book 5 (beginning only): The Jews

</br>

### The *Annals*

- Late Augustan reign to Nero (the Julio-Claudians)
- Books 1-6 survive (gap in book 5); as do Books 11-16
- 18 books total
- Annals: year by year; Consuls, omens *res internae* and *res externae*;
	- Consuls often put into the *ablative absolute* - this is unusual when dating; consuls can be removed from a sentence and the sentence still make sense - consuls irrelevant?

</br>

- Tacitus acknowledges that his subject matter is 'boring' or uninteresting - however, he has dulled this down disingenuously - he dedicates a large portion of his work to large scale military campaigns, the deeds of kings and leaders, etc.

### Polybius

- Born in Megalopolis
- Lived c. 200-c.118 BCE
- His father, Lycortas, was a *strategos* of the Achaean League
- Wrote a history of Rome's rise to world power

</br>

### How *not* to write history

- Spending too much time in the Library: Timaeus Book 12
- Sensationalism: see Phylarchus below

> [!quote]
>In his eagerness to arouse the pity and attention of his readers he treats us to a picture of clinging women with their hair dishevelled and their breasts bare, or again of crowds of both sexes together with their children and aged parents weeping and lamenting as they are led away to slavery. This sort of thing he keeps up throughout his history, always trying to bring horrors vividly before our eyes. Leaving aside the ignoble and womanish character of such a treatment of his subject, let us consider how far it is proper or serviceable to history
> 

</br>

### Requirements for a historian

- Reading memoirs, documents etc  
- Going to look yourself 
- Personal experience of war and politics

</br>

### The **Purpose** of History

- Practical value, both for the politician and for anyone trying to cope with the vicissitudes of fortune; knowledge of the past helps in the present (1.1).  
- Includes practical advice: e.g. ensuring ladders of correct height for a siege and fire signals

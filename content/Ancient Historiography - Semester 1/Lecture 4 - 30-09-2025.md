## Thucydides

- Born c. 460 BCE into an aristocratic family
- *Strategos* in 425 BCE against the Spartan forces at Amphipolis (coast of Thrace)
- Ostracised in 423 BCE after military defeat and exiled for 20 years
- Returned to Athens in 404 BCE
- Died c. 400 BCE
- Contemporary of the events he describes, and also an active participant in them

</br>


Following Homer, war becomes the primary subject for much historiography - Thucydides himself largely focussed on the Peloponnesian war, as did Herodotus

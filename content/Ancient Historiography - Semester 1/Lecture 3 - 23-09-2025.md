### Herodotus

- 'Father of Historiography' - Cicero
- From Halicarnassos (Turkey)
- Lived in the 5<sup>th</sup> century BCE
- Son of Lyxes and Dyro, related to the mythical founder of Ionia, Panyassis
- Exiled by Lygdamis to Samos, where he learned the Ionian dialect of Greek which he used in his *Histories*
- Subsequently returned to Halicarnassos and helped expel Lygdamis
- Exiled again in c. 447 CBE to Athens
- Assumed (although debated) that he travelled throughout the Eastern Mediterranean to Thrace, Phoenicia, Egypt, Mesopotamia, and Southern Italy
- Died at an uncertain date either at Athens, Thurioio or Pella in Macedonia

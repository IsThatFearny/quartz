### Reading History 'against the grain'

'Reading against the grain' in History is a term that has been popularised by historian Ann Laura Stoler. The idea behind it is that if we simply read sources in ways they were intended to be read, at some level this can reproduce the frameworks, ideologies and assumptions of the people who author the sources. To 'read against the grain' entails **identifying** and **pushing back** against such things

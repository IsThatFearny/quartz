### 'The silencing of the Haitian revolution in historical writing' introduction

**Thesis:** The Haitian revolution has been depicted by many 19th-century historians as a spontaneous, disorganised rebellion against innocent victims (i.e. the colonisers). This is not the case.

**Context:** Depiction of the rebellion in popular literature and imagery was highly biased towards white coloniser sentiment, and the legacy of colonisation caused many contemporary and near-future
Western historians to ignore or lessen the impact of the Haitian revolution and to erase its legacy and importance within the eventual downfall of the transatlantic slave trade.

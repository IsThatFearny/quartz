# Historians Toolkit Tutorial 5


**What:** Newspaper article from left-wing LGBT newspaper, *The Los Angeles Advocate*
**Who:** Don Jackson (gay man who did not witness Stonewall)
**When:** October 1969
**Target Audience:** Readers of the *Los Angeles Advocate*, those with leftist and homosexual sympathies 
**What does the document show?:** The document shows that Stonewall was not the only catalyst for the growth in the civil rights movement, and also that there were varying opinions within the gay community on how persecution should be dealt with. He also shows that he does not think that the perceived 'coalition' between various civil rights movement existed to the extent that it was theorised.

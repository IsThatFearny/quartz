## The Colonial Backdrop

- Declaration of Independence instantly makes native americans scapegoats by accusing them of being on the side of the British
- First British Colonies: Jamestown 1607; Plymouth 1620
- Thirteen colonies appeared over the next 100 years
- British authority gradually asserted itself throughout the 17th century

</br>

- Mainland colonies in British North America grew at approx. 0.5% per annum during the 18th century. By 1775 the colonies' GDP was 40% the size of Britain's, largely owing to the surge in population and transatlantic trade
- Population: 600,000, inc. 280,000 African slaves; 200,000 Scots-Irish; 85,000 Germans

</br>

### Religion

- Puritanism did not thrive beyond the 17th century, but non-conformism amongst the colonists did
- 'The Great Awakening' was a religious revival that swept the colonies in the 1730s-40s
